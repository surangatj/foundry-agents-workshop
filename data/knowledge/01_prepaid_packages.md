## Reloads and emergency credit

- Minimum reload: LKR 20. Reloads are possible through any retailer, the MyCare
  app, bank apps, and mobile wallets.
- **Emergency credit ("Loan Me")**: eligible customers (connection older than 90
  days, average monthly reload ≥ LKR 300) can borrow LKR 50 or LKR 100 by dialling
  `#356#`. A service fee of LKR 5 (for 50) or LKR 9 (for 100) is deducted with the
  next reload.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can I convert my prepaid connection to postpaid?** Yes. Visit any Care Centre
with your NIC, or request it in the MyCare app. The mobile number is kept.

**What happens when my package expires?** Data usage is charged at the base
rate of LKR 3.50 per MB until a new package is activated. Customers can block
out-of-bundle data in the MyCare app (Settings → Data Saver).
