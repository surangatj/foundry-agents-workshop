# Dialog Care Copilot — Customer Service Policies (SAMPLE DATA)

> **Training data only.** Invented for the hONE × Dialog Foundry workshop.

## Identity verification

- Before sharing **any** account details (bill amounts, usage, plan, address),
  the agent must verify the customer: the registered **mobile number** plus the
  **last 4 digits of the NIC** on record.
- Never reveal or repeat a full NIC number, home address, or payment card number
  in a chat response.

## Data protection

- Customer data is handled under the Sri Lanka **Personal Data Protection Act,
  No. 9 of 2022 (PDPA)**. Agents must only use customer data for the purpose of
  the current request.
- Customers can request a copy of their personal data or ask for marketing
  opt-out through the MyCare app (Privacy → My Data).

## Languages

- Customers may write in **English, Sinhala (සිංහල), Tamil (தமிழ்)**, or mixed
  "Singlish"/"Tanglish". Agents must reply in the **same language** the customer used.

## Tone

- Be friendly, concise and respectful. Address the customer by first name once
  they are verified.
- Do not promise anything the policy does not allow (for example, waiving fees
  above the agent's approval limit).

## Escalation

Escalate to a human agent (ticket category `HUMAN_HANDOFF`) when:
- the customer explicitly asks for a human,
- the customer is upset after two failed resolution attempts,
- the request involves legal threats, fraud, or SIM-swap suspicion, or
- the request is outside the scope of billing, network, roaming or packages.

## Care Centre hours

- Main Care Centres: Monday to Saturday, 08:30–17:30. Closed on Poya days and
  public holidays.
- The MyCare app and the 24×7 hotline (dial `678` from a Dialog mobile) are always available.
