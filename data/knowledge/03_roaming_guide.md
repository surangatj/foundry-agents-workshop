# Dialog Care Copilot — International Roaming Guide (SAMPLE DATA)

> **Training data only.** Invented for the hONE × Dialog Foundry workshop.
> NOT real Dialog Axiata roaming rates or partners.

## Activating roaming

- **Postpaid**: roaming must be activated before travel through the MyCare app or
  by dialling `#125#`. A security deposit of LKR 10,000 applies for connections
  younger than 6 months (waived on Blaster Elite).
- **Prepaid**: roaming is enabled by default. Customers need at least LKR 500
  balance before travelling.
- Customers should keep **VoLTE roaming** enabled on their handset where supported.

## Bill shock protection

- A free SMS alert is sent at LKR 5,000, 10,000 and 20,000 of roaming usage per bill cycle.
- Roaming data is **automatically stopped at LKR 25,000** of pay-as-you-go
  usage. The customer can lift the cap by replying `YES` to the alert SMS.

## Common roaming problems

1. **No signal after landing**: switch flight mode on and off, then select the
   network manually (Settings → Mobile network → Network operators).
2. **Calls work but data does not**: make sure *Data roaming* is ON and the APN
   is set to `dialogbb`.
3. **eSIM users**: the eSIM profile must remain enabled; do not delete it while abroad.
